Advanced Protein Pipeline Analysis & Visualization

Welcome to ProtPipe

ProtPipe is an interactive R Shiny application designed to streamline the analysis of proteomics data. Upload your datasets, perform statistical analysis, and generate publication-ready visualizations instantly.

Launch ProtPipe App →

(Opens in a new tab)

Key Features

📊 Data Visualization

Interactive heatmaps, volcano plots, and PCA plots designed for exploring complex protein datasets.

🧬 Pipeline Processing

Automated workflows for normalization, imputation, and statistical testing.

📂 Export Results

Download your processed data and figures directly for use in presentations and papers.
